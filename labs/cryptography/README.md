# Cryptography coursework

Topics practiced during university study include:

- Greatest common divisor and modular inverses.
- Public-key and symmetric-key algorithm concepts.
- Digital signatures, hashes and message authentication codes.
- RSA homework calculations and elliptic-curve point operations.

One worked modular-arithmetic example: the inverse of 2 modulo 17 is 9 because `2 × 9 = 18 ≡ 1 (mod 17)`.

These are coursework and mathematical exercises. They do not represent cryptographic implementation, cryptanalysis research or an independent security assessment.

Original solved worksheets are not included in this portfolio.
