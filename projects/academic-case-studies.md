# Academic case studies and assignments

## Campus Study-Room Reservation

**Course:** IS413.  
**Deliverable:** DFD modeling assignment.

Modeled a context-level reservation system with Student and Library Staff, then decomposed reservation processing into checking room availability, recording the reservation and sending confirmation. Identified Study-Room Information and Reservations as data stores.

Source: `Study_Room_DFD_Slide_Style_Final.pdf`. This is analysis/modeling, not a reservation application.

## WooCommerce and PayPal

**Context:** Team application-integration case study with Aljwharah Alfares.  
**Deliverable:** Research report, 8 November 2026.

Analyzed how the WooCommerce PayPal Payments connector relates store orders to PayPal Checkout, REST API requests and webhook status updates. Covered create/approval/capture flow, exchanged data, order synchronization and handling delayed or inconsistent notifications.

Source: `Application_Integration_Case_Study_WooCommerce_PayPal.pdf`. The work studies the documented integration; it does not claim a deployed store or processing real transactions.
